Analytical Map Catalogue

A collection of analytical maps showcasing my work in GIS, spatial analysis, and geographic visualization. These maps explore patterns, relationships, and change across different places and datasets, with an emphasis on using maps to communicate meaningful spatial stories.

01 / 07

IDW and SRTM Elevation Model Comparison Map

This map is a comparison of the SRTM elevation model from Google Earth Engine with an elevation surface created using IDW interpolation with a K4 power setting. The absolute difference map highlights the areas where the interpolated surface differs most from the original SRTM elevation data, allowing the accuracy and overall similarity of the two models to be evaluated.

ArcGIS Pro · Google Earth Engine · Geographic Visualization · 2026

02 / 07

Regularized and Tension Spline Model Comparison Map

This map is another comparison of the original SRTM elevation model but instead with surfaces created using Regularized and Tension Spline interpolation. The maps demonstrate how different spline methods produce different patterns and elevation ranges, showing how the choice of interpolation technique can influence the resulting surface and its representation of terrain.

ArcGIS Pro · Google Earth Engine · Geographic Visualization · 2026

03 / 07

SRTM and Kriging Model Comparison Map

Another comparison of the SRTM elevation model, now with a Kriging interpolation surface and its corresponding Kriging variance. The Kriging model estimates elevation across the study area, while the variance map shows the level of uncertainty associated with those estimates. Together, the maps demonstrate both the predicted elevation surface and where the interpolation is more or less reliable.

ArcGIS Pro · Google Earth Engine · Geographic Visualization · 2026

04 / 07

Bicknell Thrush Habitat Map

This map shows Bicknell’s Thrush occurrence records from 1999–2019 and their relationship to elevation. GBIF sighting data were mapped as points and compared with elevation data from a Digital Elevation Model (DEM) from OpenTopography. The DEM was resampled to a 200 × 200 meter resolution so that it could be used with the other data in the analysis. Looking at the sightings alongside elevation helps show the types of landscapes where the species thrives.

GIS · Spatial Analysis · DEM · 2026

05 / 07

D.C. Bivariate MHI and Veteran Percent Map

This map shows the relationship between the percentage of veterans and median household income across ZIP Codes in the Washington, D.C. Nielsen Designated Market Area. A bivariate choropleth map was used to display both variables at the same time, with different colors representing their combined values. This makes it easier to compare areas with higher or lower veteran populations alongside differences in household income and identify patterns between the two variables.

GIS · Spatial Analysis · 2026

06 / 07

Behavioral Disorder Deaths Local Morans I Map

This map shows the spatial patterns of behavioral disorder death rates across counties in the study area. It highlights areas where counties with similar or different death rates are grouped together, making it easier to see clusters and unusual counties. The map was created using Local Moran’s I and Queen’s Contiguity to examine these patterns.

GIS · Spatial Analysis · Local Moran's I · 2026

07 / 07

Behavioral Disorder Deaths Getis-Ord Gi* Map

This map shows the locations of statistically significant hot spots and cold spots for behavioral disorder death rates. The red areas represent clusters of higher death rates, while the blue areas represent clusters of lower death rates. The map was created using the Getis-Ord Gi* method with a 70.26-mile distance band to examine the overall clustering pattern.

GIS · Spatial Analysis · Getis-Ord Gi* · 2026
